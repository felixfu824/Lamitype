# Lamitype v0.5.16

## 繁體中文

說出口的金額、比例與時間，直接以文件和試算表慣用的數字格式輸入。

- **數字轉換更完整。** 中文（繁體／簡體）、英文與中英混用都適用，涵蓋金額、百分比、帶單位的小數、時間、日期、電話、版本與型號。例如「四萬五千塊」→「4.5萬塊」、「一億兩千萬」→「1.2億」、「三點半開會」→「3:30開會」、「iPhone十七Pro」→「iPhone17Pro」。
- **日常說法維持原樣。** 「千萬不要」、「一個人」、「兩杯咖啡」不會被改成數字。轉換在本地完成。
- **即時預覽可直接貼進試算表。** Google Sheets 等試算表軟體原本顯示「已略過自動貼上」的情境，現在會貼上最終文字（偵測到切換 App、視窗或分頁，或游標在密碼欄位時，仍不會自動貼上）。

**更新方式：** 退出 Lamitype，從 DMG 將 `Lamitype.app` 拖到 Applications，取代同一位置的 App，再重新開啟。設定與已下載的模型會保留。

## English

Spoken amounts, percentages and times now arrive in the number formats you would type in documents and spreadsheets.

- **More complete number conversion.** Works in Mandarin (Traditional and Simplified), English and mixed Chinese-English, covering amounts, percentages, decimals with units, times, dates, phone numbers, versions and model codes: "forty-five thousand dollars" → "$45,000", "twelve point five percent" → "12.5%", "three thirty pm" → "3:30 pm", 「三點半開會」→「3:30開會」.
- **Everyday wording stays as words.** Idioms, small everyday counts and names such as "Twenty One Pilots" are left alone. Conversion runs on your Mac for both local and cloud dictation.
- **Live preview pastes into spreadsheets.** Google Sheets and similar grids that used to show "Automatic paste skipped" now receive the final text. Detected app, window or tab changes, and password fields, still block automatic paste.

**Update:** quit Lamitype, drag `Lamitype.app` from the DMG into Applications and replace the app at the same location, then relaunch. Settings and downloaded models are preserved.
